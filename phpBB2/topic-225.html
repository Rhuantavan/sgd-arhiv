Vsekakor se da. Samo sledi navodilom tule:
http://www.3dlevel.com/artificialcommunity.php

Ob igri pisem tutorial in dajem celoten source po delih ven, kot se razvija, da lahko sproti spremljas razvoj. Na koncu bo to tutorial kako izdelat en tak 'manjsi' gejm od zacetka do konca, seveda z uporabo nasega engina. Zaenkrat lahko dobis kratek design doc, vse sound effecte in glasbo, nekaj arta.

Da ne bom prevec govoru, tle je en odsek s tega Game Creation Tutorial foruma:








Citiram:
Now that this community counts quite some interested programmers we are going to start the complete game creation tutorial we mentioned in the news.

This is a good opportunity for beginner programmers, because we will cover game creation from scratch to completion. Also all the design, art, and sounds/music will be provided from our team so you can focus on programming.

This tutorial is also useful to advanced programmers who are just starting to use our engines. While Kavan's lessons in the specific forums will cover all functionality of both our engines, in here I will show how to use that knowledge to create a complete game.

At the start, we will cover a simple game as is advised for beginnings. The best is to go with a remake of a classic arcade and I decided to create yet another space invaders clone.



Drugac pa joinajte in lahko spremljajte zadevo. Zaenkrat smo cist na zacetku tko da lahko sproti lepo spremljate razvoj ene take igre.
